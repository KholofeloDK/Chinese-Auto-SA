# Chinese Auto Sales Analysis - South Africa

Analysis of Chinese automotive brands (BYD, Geely, Chery, GWM, BAIC) in the South African market.
Focus on EV transition, regional performance, and revenue vs units.

> 300 synthetic transactions modeled on NAAMSA trends 2023-2024 across 9 provinces.

### Key Insights
- BYD leads with ~36% market share - EV dominance (Dolphin, Atto 3, Seal)
- Gauteng = 35% of all sales (Johannesburg/Pretoria EV hub)
- Western Cape leads Hybrid adoption
- Petrol still 65% of units, but EV + Hybrid growing fast
- GWM Tank 300 highest ASP at ~R750k

### Visuals
- Units vs Revenue by Brand
- Sales by Fuel Type (Petrol/Hybrid/EV)
- Regional Heatmap: Brand x Province
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Fuel Mix by Province (%)

### How to Run
pip install -r requirements.txt
jupyter notebook notebooks/analysis.ipynb

### Tech Stack
Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib
